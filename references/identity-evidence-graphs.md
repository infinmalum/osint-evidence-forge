# Identity Evidence Graphs (Authorized Investigations)

Derived from organized-team identity investigations (undercover-unit class).
Load ONLY for investigations that pass the Safety and Authorization Boundary:
verified official listing, sanctioned actor, or clear law-enforcement/safeguarding
mandate. Applies to the subject only — never to family, friends, or associates
who are not themselves qualifying subjects.

## Identity resolution primitives

These are structural methods for linking an alias to a person, in ascending
strength:

1. **Alias consistency** — one persona reused across platforms with coherent
   age, biography, and photo sets; note that cover identities often have a
   fabricated birth year offset from the real one.
2. **Document-series anomalies** — passport numbers in sequential blocks,
   registration addresses clustering in military towns, issue dates aligned
   with unit deployment patterns. Single anomalies suggest; clusters argue.
3. **Travel-pattern joins** — flight/hotel/rental records showing the alias
   and the real person never contradicting each other, or concurrent trips
   by multiple team members to the same city before an incident.
4. **Organizational clustering** — shared addresses (same apartment building,
   same award-timing), shared travel companions, shared unit references
   linking individuals into one team.
5. **Official-registry cross-checks** — diplomatic accreditation lists,
   employer confirmations, sports/race sign-ups, archived official pages
   against their live versions (a person absent from archived registries but
   present in live ones is itself a finding).
6. **Independent corroboration** — a second outlet, an official confirmation,
   or a leaked registry — only counts if it derives from a different
   fact-source, not the same document.

## Joins discipline

- Every join states which two identifiers it connects and its fact-source.
- Circumstantial joins (same city, same week) are compatibility, not identity;
  apply the +2/+1/0/-1/-2 effect scale.
- The strongest identity link is a closed loop: alias → document → registry →
  independent confirmation → back to alias. A chain that never closes is a
  lead, not an identification.
- "Recognized him from the photo" testimony is weak; without biometric match
  or documentary link it stays 0.

## What this does NOT unlock

- Biometric identification stays external and authorized (see SKILL.md).
- No investigation of family, friends, or associates beyond what the authorized
  mandate requires; their privacy posture remains that of private individuals.
- No publishing of real-time location; route findings to responsible authorities.
- Public exposure still requires editorial-ethics review: what is published
  must serve the public interest, not doxxing dressed as accountability.

## Attribution hygiene

- State-actor investigations attract sophisticated counter-narratives: expect
  forged documents, recycled disinformation, and personas designed to be
  "found". Verify documents against issuing-authority archives.
- Leaked/obtained registries carry their own provenance questions; record how
  each was obtained and by whom.
- Prefer convergence of many independent weak links over one dramatic strong
  link; dramatic single documents warrant extra skepticism.
